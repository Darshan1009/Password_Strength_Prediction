Password Strength Tester

Having a strong password helps keep your accounts safe and secure.

  • Check the strength of a password based on its complexity and how easily it can be guessed.
  • See how long it would take for a computer to crack it.
  • Get tips to improve your password security.

How to Use

  1. Enter a password you want to test.
  2. See the result and tips to improve your password.
  3. If your password is weak, try the suggested strong password!
Tip: Longer passphrases are much harder to crack than short, complex passwords!

What Makes a Good Password?

  • Make it long: Use 12+ characters or a passphrase
  • Make it unpredictable: Avoid patterns, names, or dates
  • Make it unique: Never reuse passwords
Example: glowering-armour-permanently-jacketS-@$73
